Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you book an appointment, submit feedback, or contact Pehchan Unisex Salon, we collect personal information such as your name, mobile phone number, email address, appointment date/time preferences, and any specific styling notes or allergies you choose to disclose.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data exclusively to:

  • Schedule and manage your salon appointments conflict-free.
  • Transmit automated appointment confirmations, reminders, and schedule updates via WhatsApp and Email.
  • Maintain your visit history to personalize hair color formulas and skincare treatment logs.
  • Comply with tax and accounting regulations regarding invoice generation.

3. Zero Third-Party Selling

Pehchan Unisex Salon maintains a strict zero-spam and zero-reselling policy. We never sell, rent, or trade your contact information with external marketers or advertising agencies.

4. Security of Your Information

All appointment details and records are stored behind encrypted server infrastructures with role-based administrative access controls. Sensitive credentials and API tokens are never exposed in client-facing browser scripts.

5. Contacting Our Data Officer

If you have any questions or wish to request data deletion, contact us at privacy@pehchansalon.com or at our salon address: Shop 104-106, Luxury Promenade, High Street Avenue.
