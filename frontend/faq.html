Answers & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our salon etiquette, slot booking system, cancellations, and products.

How does your online booking system work?

Our online booking engine checks our real-time master schedule in seconds. You select your service, choose an available date and time slot, pick your preferred specialist (optional), and receive an instant reference code along with automated WhatsApp and email confirmations. No upfront credit card is needed.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes. If your plans change, we kindly ask for at least 2 hours' notice so another client can utilize the slot. You can reschedule by reaching us on WhatsApp at +91 98765 43210 with your booking reference code.

Are your hair and skin products safe and certified?

We use 100% genuine, authorized salon-grade products including L'Oréal Professional, Olaplex, Moroccanoil, and O3+ Derma care. All keratin treatments are formaldehyde-free, and hair dyes are ammonia-free to safeguard your scalp and hair health.

What should I expect for a bridal makeup consultation?

Our lead bridal artist conducts an in-depth 45-minute consultation reviewing your outfits, jewelry, lighting conditions of your venue, and skin undertones. We offer trial sessions so you can experience the exact HD airbrush look before your wedding date.

What payment methods do you accept at the salon?

We accept all major payment methods upon service completion: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash. Instant digital GST tax invoices are provided.

What are your hygiene and sterilization protocols?

Our salon operates under clinical hygiene standards. Metallic instruments undergo hospital-grade dry-heat autoclaving and UV sterilization. Towels, neck strips, and pedicure liners are 100% disposable and replaced after every single guest.
