Salon Etiquette

Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Arrival & Punctuality

To ensure every guest enjoys unhurried pampering, please arrive 5 to 10 minutes prior to your scheduled time slot. In the event of a delay exceeding 15 minutes without notification, we may need to shorten or reschedule your session to respect subsequent guests.

2. Rescheduling & Cancellations

We understand schedules change. We request a courtesy notice of at least 2 hours for cancellations or rescheduling. You can adjust your slot via WhatsApp or telephone by referencing your booking code.

3. Patch Test & Chemical Treatments

For extensive chemical treatments such as permanent hair color, bleaching, or intensive chemical peels, we recommend a preliminary 24-hour skin patch test, especially for first-time clients or sensitive skin types.

4. Pricing & Payment Settlement

All prices listed on our menu and website are inclusive of applicable goods and services taxes (GST). Payment is settled upon service completion at our reception desk via card, UPI, or cash.

5. Bridal & Event Booking Policy

For full bridal and groom makeover packages involving private VIP dressing suites, advance slot confirmation or pre-booking consultation is required to reserve the master artist team exclusively for your auspicious date.

6. Satisfaction Commitment

If you are not completely delighted with any element of your haircut or styling, please inform our art director within 48 hours and we will happily make any necessary refinements with our compliments.
